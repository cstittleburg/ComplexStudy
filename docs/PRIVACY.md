This policy explains what Shift Ready (the "Service"), operated by [Operator legal name]
("we", "us"), collects about you, why, and what you can do about it. It is written to be
read, not skimmed. If anything is unclear, email [contact email].

## 1. The short version

- We collect your email (to sign you in), the course materials you upload (to build your
  study content), and a record of how you answer practice items (to adapt the Service to
  you and to see whether it works).
- **Your uploaded materials and the content generated from them are private to you.** No
  other user sees them. We do not sell them or use them to build content for others.
- We send extracted text from your materials to Anthropic's AI service to generate study
  content. Anthropic does not train on it.
- We use aggregate, anonymous statistics (for example, "students in this course miss
  prioritisation questions most") to improve the Service. These contain none of your
  materials and cannot identify you.
- You can export or delete your data at any time.

## 2. What we collect

| What | Why | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Email address** | To sign you in (we use one-time email links, no passwords) and to contact you about the Service | You, at sign-up |
| **Uploaded materials** (files you add to a course, and the text we extract from them) | To generate your study content | You |
| **Course setup** (course names, materials list, study methods and frameworks you chose, notes you wrote) | To organise your study content | You |
| **Study activity** (which items you answered, your score, time taken, whether it was a retry, the study mode, the date) | To track your progress, adapt which formats and topics you see, and show you the Insights page | Generated as you use the Service |
| **Ratings and feedback** (one-tap ratings after a session, free-text feedback, "this item is wrong" flags) | To improve the Service and fix bad content | You |
| **Technical data** (browser type, approximate region from IP address, error logs) | To keep the Service running and fix bugs | Your browser and our hosting provider |

We do **not** collect: your name (unless you put it in an email to us), your grades from
your program, your student ID, payment details (there is no paid plan during the pilot),
or precise location. We do not use advertising trackers.

## 3. How we use it

- **To provide the Service:** store your materials, generate study content from them,
  save your progress, sync it across your devices.
- **To adapt the Service to you:** the Service uses your study activity to decide which
  practice formats and topics to show you next. This is automated. It affects only what
  study content you see; it does not make any decision about you with legal or similarly
  significant effect.
- **To improve the Service:** we look at aggregate statistics across users (for example,
  which item types are missed most in a course, or whether a feature is used). These
  statistics do not include your materials or anything that identifies you. Where we
  compare groups, each group has at least [five] people.
- **To communicate with you:** service messages (sign-in links, important changes,
  security notices). We do not send marketing email during the pilot. If that changes,
  you will be able to opt out.
- **To keep the Service safe:** detecting abuse, enforcing the Terms of Service.

## 4. What we do not do

- We do not sell your data.
- We do not share your uploaded materials or generated content with other users.
- We do not use your materials to generate content for anyone else.
- We do not share your data with your university, your program, or your instructors.
- We do not use your data to train AI models, and neither does our AI provider.

## 5. Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers to run the Service. Each processes data only
to provide their service to us, under their own privacy commitments.

| Provider | What they process | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Supabase] | Your email, uploaded materials, extracted text, course setup, study activity | Accounts, database and file storage |
| [Netlify] | Technical data; requests to our server-side functions | Hosting the Service |
| [Anthropic] | Extracted text from your materials, and the prompts we build from it | Generating study content. Anthropic retains API inputs for a limited period for abuse monitoring and does not use them to train its models. See Anthropic's privacy policy. |

We do not share data with anyone else, except where the law requires it or to protect
the rights and safety of users and the Service. If we are ever acquired or merge with
another company, your data would move with the Service, under this policy, and you would
be told.

## 6. Where it is stored

## 8. Your choices and rights

- **Export.** You can copy your study activity from the Insights page at any time. You
  can request a full export of your data by emailing [contact email].
- **Delete.** You can delete individual materials or courses in the Service. You can
  delete your whole account from Settings or by email; this removes your materials,
  generated content, course setup, study activity and email within [30] days, apart from
  backups (section 7) and aggregate statistics that cannot identify you.
- **Correct.** You can change your course setup in the Service. Email us to change
  your email address.
- **Object or restrict.** If you do not want your study activity included in aggregate
  statistics, email us and we will exclude it. (The Service will still adapt to you
  individually; that is how it works.)
- **Complain.** If you think we have handled your data badly, tell us first. You may also
  have the right to complain to a data-protection authority in your region.

We will respond to requests within [30] days. We may ask you to confirm your identity
(usually by replying from your account email).

## 9. Security

Your data is protected by row-level security in our database, meaning the database
itself enforces that only your account can read your rows. Connections are encrypted.
Server-side keys are never sent to your browser. No system is perfectly secure; if we
learn of a breach affecting your data we will tell you promptly.

## 10. Children

The Service is for adults in nursing and health-sciences programs. We do not knowingly
collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has an account, email us and
we will remove it.

## 11. Educational records

The Service is not operated by or on behalf of any school. Nothing you upload or do in
the Service becomes part of your educational record at your institution, and we do not
report to your institution.

## 12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. If a change is material, we will tell you by email or in the
Service at least [14] days before it takes effect.
